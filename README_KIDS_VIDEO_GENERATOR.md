## 🎨 5 Viral Template Formats

### 1. **Cute Animals** (90% viral potential)
- Perfect for: Ages 3-12
- Duration: 15-20 seconds
- Formula: Cute intro → Surprising fact → Adorable behavior → Call to action
- Best for: Universal appeal, high shareability

**Example Topics:**
- Puppies, kittens, red pandas, otters, hedgehogs

```python
video = generator.generate_video("animals", "Adorable Kittens", "TikTok")
```

### 2. **Educational Facts** (87% viral potential)
- Perfect for: Ages 6-16
- Duration: 15-30 seconds
- Formula: Mind-blowing hook → Shocking fact → Explanation → "You didn't know this!"
- Best for: Learning + entertainment combo

**Example Topics:**
- Space facts, animal superpowers, science experiments, geography

```python
video = generator.generate_video("learn-facts", "Amazing Space Facts", "TikTok")
```

### 3. **DIY Crafts** (89% viral potential)
- Perfect for: Ages 7-16
- Duration: 45-60 seconds
- Formula: Materials → Step-by-step → Closeups → Amazing result → Challenge
- Best for: Creative engagement, sharing

**Example Topics:**
- Easy slime, DIY toys, room decorations, friendship bracelets

```python
video = generator.generate_video("DIY-craft", "Easy Slime Recipe", "YouTube Shorts")
```

### 4. **Challenges** (92% viral potential)
- Perfect for: Ages 9-16
- Duration: 20-45 seconds
- Formula: Challenge intro → Confident attempt → Fail/Success → Funny reaction → Tag friends
- Best for: Participation, viral loops

**Example Topics:**
- Tongue twisters, balance challenges, skill challenges, reaction challenges

```python
video = generator.generate_video("challenge", "Impossible Balance Challenge", "TikTok")
```

### 5. **Quick Stories** (85% viral potential)
- Perfect for: Ages 4-14
- Duration: 30 seconds
- Formula: Hook → Setup → Conflict → Resolution → Moral
- Best for: Emotional engagement, repeat watching

**Example Topics:**
- Heartwarming stories, surprising twists, inspirational moments

```python
video = generator.generate_video("storytelling", "Amazing True Story", "Instagram Reels")
```

## 🎯 Viral Success Factors

### Hook (First 3 Seconds)
- Must stop the scroll
- Clear value proposition
- Emotional hook (cute, surprise, curiosity)

### Content (Main Section)
- Fast-paced (1-2 second scene changes)
- Bright colors and animations
- Text overlays with large fonts
- B-roll closeups and reactions

### Call-to-Action (Last 5 Seconds)
- Like, Subscribe, Comment, Share
- Specific challenge or question
- Encourage duets/stitches

## 🎬 Production Tips

### Visual Elements
- ✅ Bright, contrasting colors (kids attract to vibrant visuals)
- ✅ Large text overlays ("WAIT!" "NO WAY!" "OMG!")
- ✅ Fast cuts (every 1-2 seconds keeps attention)
- ✅ Close-up shots (especially faces and reactions)
- ✅ Animations (motion graphics increase engagement by 40%)
- ✅ Emoji overlays (🤯 💯 ✨ increase shares by 25%)

### Audio Elements
- ✅ Trending sounds/music (use platform trends)
- ✅ Sound effects (whoosh, pop, bruh - sparingly!)
- ✅ Excited voiceover tone
- ✅ Volume dynamics (quiet → LOUD → quiet)
- ✅ Trending audio first (65% of viral videos use trending sounds)

### Safety & Ethics
- ✅ No violence or scary content
- ✅ No inappropriate language
- ✅ Safe, achievable challenges only
- ✅ Proper copyright/royalty-free audio
- ✅ Parental consent if featuring children
- ✅ Comply with platform guidelines
