## 4. TIMELINE ENGINE

### Core Capabilities
- Multi-track timeline (Video, Audio, Text, B-Roll tracks)
- Track management (add/remove tracks)
- Clip rendering with time-based positioning
- Playhead with scrubbing support
- Timeline zoom and pan
